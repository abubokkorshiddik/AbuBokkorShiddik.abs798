Who I Am

Research Profile

I am a researcher with a growing portfolio of peer-reviewed publications in high-impact international journals including Scientific Reports, BMJ Open, Global Epidemiology, and International Journal of Health Geographics. My research covers global disease modeling, dengue early warning systems, and climate-health interactions.

My work integrates machine learning, explainable AI (SHAP, LIME), Bayesian deep learning, and spatial analysis to support evidence-based decision-making in public health — across 194 countries using large-scale multi-source datasets.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Machine Learning in Public Health
Primary research focus — tree-based and deep learning models for disease surveillance.
Explainable AI (XAI)
SHAP, LIME, and interpretable models that make predictions auditable, not black boxes.
Epidemiological Modeling
Statistical and ML models tracing how disease burden shifts across time and place.
Spatiotemporal Analysis
Mapping disease clustering with Moran's I and Getis-Ord Gi* across regions and years.
Disease Prediction Systems
District- and national-level early warning tools for outbreak forecasting.
Climate & Health
Eco-climatic triggers linking temperature, rainfall, and vector-borne disease risk.
