Your 4-phase roadmap

PHASE 1 · DAYS 1–12

Build your foundation

Learn the test format, improve grammar and vocabulary, understand writing assessment, and practise speaking every day.

PHASE 2 · DAYS 13–48

Cambridge Books 1–9

Complete all four tests in each book. Review answers carefully and identify your weakest question types.

PHASE 3 · DAYS 49–84

Cambridge Books 10–18

Complete the remaining tests, improve accuracy, practise timed writing, and refine your speaking responses.

PHASE 4 · DAYS 85–90

Final review and readiness

Focus on recurring mistakes, timed practice, weak skills, and your exam-day strategy.

Study smart: There are 72 test sets across Cambridge Books 1–18. This plan schedules one set each day on Days 13–84. Review every answer and keep an error log. Older books remain useful for practice, but prioritise recent tests when judging readiness for the current IELTS format.

Cambridge IELTS Academic Books

Work through Books 1–18 in ascending order. Each book contains four tests in this plan.

Use the original answer keys and audio supplied with your books. Follow the instructions and time limits for each test.

Suggested daily routine

Aim for approximately 5 hours 30 minutes of focused work. Take short breaks between sessions.

60 MINUTES

Listening

Practise audio, predict answers, identify distractors, and review transcript mistakes.

60 MINUTES

Reading

Practise skimming, scanning, locating evidence, and managing question-specific timing.

75 MINUTES

Writing

Practise Task 1 or Task 2. Review task response, organisation, vocabulary, and grammar.

45 MINUTES

Speaking

Record answers, practise Parts 1–3, and review fluency, clarity, vocabulary, and grammar.

45 MINUTES

Grammar and vocabulary

Study your supplementary books and review your personal error log.

45 MINUTES

Review and reflection

Check answers, classify errors, revise difficult words, and plan the next session.

On full Cambridge test days, prioritise the timed sections and thorough review. You do not need to write a complete essay and practise every speaking part from scratch every day; alternate these activities when necessary.
