How pricing works

The displayed amounts are starting prices, not fixed prices. Final quotations consider dataset size, data quality, research objectives, analytical complexity, number of deliverables, reporting requirements and timeline. A project involving several simple analyses may cost less than a project involving one highly complex analytical workflow.
